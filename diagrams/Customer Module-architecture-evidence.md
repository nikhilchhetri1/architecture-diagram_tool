## Open Items / Not Verified

- The RDS database is clearly accessed through PostgreSQL clients and RDS Proxy, but the engine version, cluster/instance topology, and owning infrastructure stack were not found.
- The GCDB Customer API's owning repository and its data engine were not identified.
- Keycloak's deployment platform and version were not identified.
- The shared custom Lambda authorizer's implementation and the shared Cognito user-pool stack were not inspected.
- `prod`, `prod2`, and `prod3` environment files differ in optional operational settings. Optional New Relic and Kinesis/Firehose-related configuration is not represented as a generic production component.
- No direct application-code evidence was found for current Customer runtime publication to SNS, SQS, EventBridge, or Kinesis; no messaging/event-pipeline layer is shown.
- The CloudFront template declares DR origins for certain environments, but its origin-group failover block is commented out; DR failover is intentionally not shown as active.
- The `UpdateCustomerInfo → Core Customer API` and `GetCustomerAddress → Core Customer API` edges are drawn dashed (Inferred) because the specific outbound-URL code for those two handlers was not individually re-read; they are inferred from the confirmed `CreateCustomerRepository` `racApiUrl` forwarding pattern used elsewhere in the same BFF codebase.
- The Enterprise ESS Gateway (weblead/refund status), RAC Refund API, and CMS/Mule Address Doctor endpoints are drawn as distinct external systems because their credentials, base URLs, and request shapes are each configured and evidenced separately in `GetWebLeadsRepository.ts`, `SalesLeadRefundRepository.ts`, and `ValidateAddressRepository.ts` — none of their owning services/repos were reviewed.
